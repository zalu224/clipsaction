Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers the Clipping app, a private tool run by the ClipsAction channel on its owner's own computer.

What it accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, Clipping asks for:

  • Google Sheets, to read and update the owner's own "Clipping Queue" spreadsheet, which lists videos to clip and the clips made.
  • YouTube, to upload clips to the owner's own channel and to read public video and channel information.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to make and upload clips for the owner. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The Google sign-in token and working files (audio, transcripts, rendered clips) stay on the owner's computer.
  • Clipping uses only public stream and video information from creators. It doesn't collect personal data from viewers or other users.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Clipping's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and by deleting the local token file.
