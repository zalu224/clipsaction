ClipsAction Clipping

Clipping is a private tool run by the ClipsAction channel. It turns gaming streams from creators who allow clipping (paid clipping campaigns or written permission) into short vertical clips with captions, and uploads them to the ClipsAction YouTube channel.

It is used only by its owner. It doesn't offer accounts or sign-in to anyone else.
